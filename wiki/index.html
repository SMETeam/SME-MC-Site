Boss Info

Learning about them can help you prepare for battles and know what to expect when you encounter one. On weekends they have a 2x increase in spawn rate, so keep an eye out!

Dimensions Info

Survival isn't the survival you're expecting.. This server has a semi-modded experience!

Overworld

The main world, filled with custom generation, secret dungeons, and more!

Nether

Fast travel routes, but pretty dangerous.. many players tend to use the nether for hidden bases or to find quartz which gives some good cash!

End

Ender mobs and a respawning dragon. Prepare before heading in and watch for the void..

Aether

A custom sky world, great for traveling, ores, and also for farms!

Economy Info

Ah yes, money. Survival has a lot of ways to make cash, like mobs, orders, auction, season pass, shop, fishing, maybe spending on the Snubby's shop!

Mobs and Players

Some mobs and players can drop money when you kill them. Different things can pay different amounts, so you will have to figure out what is worth farming.

Orders

Orders are items that other players are looking for. If you have the item they need, deliver it and get paid.

/orders

Auction

Got something good but don't want to sell it to the normal shop? Put it on auction and let another player buy it.

/ah /ah sell [amount] [price]

Shop

The shop is the main way to sell items. Prices can go down after people sell a lot of something, so don't expect every item to stay the same forever.

/shop /sellgui

Fishing

Fishing is a chill way to make extra cash. Better and rarer fish can sell for more, so grab a rod and find some water.

Rotating Snubby's Shop

Snubby's shop has a rotating selection of items. Some stuff is rare and stock is limited, so check it before everything good is gone.

One Block

One block. A whole island to build. You mine the block, level it up, and see what turns up next! Sounds simple.. until a mob shows up while you are standing over the void...

Tip: /ob help shows the One Block commands in game if you forget one...

Getting Started

New to One Block? Do these first and you should be good to go :D

1

Get to your island

Use /join or /ob j to create your island or return to it. Need to head back to the main world? Use /ob leave or /spawn.

2

Build somewhere to stand

Mine the OneBlock and use what you get to widen your platform. Put a chest down early. You really do not want your useful drops going into the void..

3

Watch your level

The level progress bar is on while you play. Keep breaking the OneBlock to unlock different blocks, mobs, and chests as you go.

If you die, the server is set to send you back to your island. Still, maybe put some walls around that block before you get too brave!

Level up goodies

Each level gives money. Some milestones give extra money and items too: level 5 has coal, level 20 has a diamond, and level 50 has emeralds.

Chests

Chests can contain things like saplings, food, tools, ores, and other useful supplies. Save anything you might need for a farm or a future build!

Islands & Friends

You can build your island with a team or show it off to visitors. Just check who has build access before handing it out!

Team up

Invite a player with /ob invite <player>. They can use /ob accept to join. Need to remove a teammate? Use /ob kick <player>.

Visitors & building

Use /ob allow_visit to turn visits on or off. To let somebody build without joining your team, use /ob trust <player>. You can check /ob trustlist or remove access with /ob untrust <player>.

PVP

PVP is off by default on your island. The island owner can change it with /ob togglepvp. Worth checking before inviting everybody over!

Thinking about resetting?

/ob idreset resets your island data. This one is serious, so make sure you mean it before confirming anything in game.

Skills & Rebirth

Mining levels earns skill points for your island team. Open /skills and pick what helps your build most.

Mining

Improve your mining skill as you level up.

Movement

Speed and jump skills help you get around your island.

Fighting

Damage and hearts help when the OneBlock gives you a less friendly surprise.

What is rebirth?

Once your island reaches level 25, the owner can use /ob rebirth. It sends the island level back to 0 and gives a permanent multiplier for leveling up again. Your island and items stay, but your team's money, spent skills, and unspent skill points reset. Read the in game confirmation before using /rebirth confirm!

Quests & Events

Need a break from mining? There is more to do around the island.

Quests

Open /quests to find Island Journey goals, Daily Jobs, Weekly Challenges, and Crate Quests. The journey teaches the OneBlock progression loop, so that is a good place to start!

Shop & auction

Sell extra resources with /shop or /sellgui. You can also browse player listings with /ah. Check /balance before spending everything :P

/shop/sellgui/ah

Surprise events

Watch chat and the action bar for events. Mob Invasion sends hostile mobs to islands, Lucky Streak adds bonus drops, and XP Storm and Money Rain reward mining. Meteor Shower sends items to spawn, so head over when it starts!

Weekend boost

Saturday and Sunday have a 2x multiplier boost. Good time to get some extra levels in!

Enchanting

Want better gear? The Wizard and Tinkerer are at spawn!

Enchanting Wizard

Right-click the Wizard to buy random custom enchant books with XP. Right-click a book to see what you got. Check its success chance before using it!

The Tinkerer

Got a custom enchant book you do not want? Trade it with the Tinkerer for Secret Dust! Open the dust for a chance at Magic Dust, which can boost a book's success chance.

Use /spawn to find them. The Tinkerer also takes custom enchanted gear.

Crates & Keys

Crates have extra rewards for your island. Just grab a key first!

Common

Finish the Daily Supply Run quest for a Common Key. You can do it again after 24 hours.

Rare

Finish Midweek Grind for a Rare Key. It resets after 3 days and 12 hours.

Legendary

Finish Legendary Ascension for a Legendary Key. This one resets after 7 days.

How do I get a key?

Open /quests and pick Crate Quests. Some Journey quests give keys too! You can also get them from the shop page.

Crates are at spawn. Use /spawn, then check the rewards before opening one!
